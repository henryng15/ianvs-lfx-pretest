The fix is right. `getfullargspec()` splits positional and keyword-only parameters, and
the original only consults `.args`, so keyword-only parameters are dropped. Confirmed by
running the current helper:

```text
parse_kwargs(positional, threshold=0.9) -> {'threshold': 0.9}
parse_kwargs(kwonly,     threshold=0.9) -> {}
```

Handling `varkw` under a non-`kwargs` name as well as `kwonlyargs` is a genuine
improvement over the narrower reading of #597.
